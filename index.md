<section class="bio" markdown="1">

I'm a research scientist at [Patronus AI](https://www.patronus.ai). My current primary focus is **text-based world modeling**: language models that simulate an environment's dynamics, so agents can train on diverse, on-demand experience instead of hand-built tasks. My new [NeurIPS 2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16204) shows that masked diffusion language models make stronger, more steerable world models than autoregressive ones, and transfer zero-shot to unseen environments.

Many of my ideas come from cognitive psychology: what drives LLM behaviors and nuances, how people hold mental states, how and when information retrieval is required, and revision of beliefs as evidence arrives. That runs through my work on agent memory ([MemTrack](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01353)), tip-of-the-tongue retrieval ([BLUR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19193), [DETOUR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00352)), finding where agents fail in long traces ([TRAIL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08638)), and [TRACE-ing reward hacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20103) in code environments.

<h2>Before this</h2>

At USC's [Information Sciences Institute](https://www.isi.edu) I was advised by [Filip Ilievski](https://www.ilievski.info) and [Fred Morstatter](https://www.isi.edu/directory/fredmors/) on argumentation: identifying logical fallacies robustly and explainably, and grounding argument-quality judgments in background knowledge.
